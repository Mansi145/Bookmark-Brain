## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-27

First release, covering Phases 0–2 of the [plan](docs/PLAN.md).

### Added

- Sign in with Google.
- A browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox that syncs your bookmarks and keeps them up to date. Connect it with one click from its popup. It asks only for the bookmarks, storage and alarms permissions, plus access to Bookmark Brain's own server.
- One bookmark per page, even when the same page is saved in several browsers.
- The feed as a newspaper front page: Today, This week and Earlier, with the newest unread stories biggest and a preview image on the top story.
- Numbered pages for Earlier.
- All, Unread and Read views, and filters by browser and folder.
- A Needs sign-in section for pages behind an account or paywall, and a Dead links section for pages that no longer load.
- Find archived copy, which looks up a dead link on the Wayback Machine.
- AI summaries with key points, made on demand from the page's text, its archived copy, or its title and description.
- A bookmark detail panel with the summary, key points, Open page, Mark as read / unread and Re-summarise.
- A daily summary limit per user, and a Limit reached dialog that can let the maintainer know.
- An account menu with Settings, a Dark mode switch and Sign out.
- Settings to connect browsers, see and revoke connected ones, download your data as a JSON file, and delete your account.
- Light and dark themes. The app follows your system until you choose one.
- A logo that animates while a summary is being made, a page loads, or the Wayback Machine is checked.
- Sign-up and How it works pages.
